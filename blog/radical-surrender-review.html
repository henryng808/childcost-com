Named by Penguin Random House among the most anticipated nonfiction releases of fall 2026, Radical Surrender is "the perfect book for anyone seeking stronger family and work relationships, more peace." At a moment when parental burnout is at epidemic levels and the care economy strains under unprecedented pressure, this book makes a counterintuitive case: that surrender — not control — is the gateway to stronger families and more sustainable work lives.

The Surrender Premium

The author identifies the "surrender premium" — the compounding return of releasing our grip on the illusion that we can control every outcome of family life. In a 2026 world where parents are sold a relentless message of optimization, Radical Surrender is a corrective. It offers a practical framework for choosing peace over perfection — in our homes and at our desks — and for modeling that choice for our children.

Lessons for Families:

  • Peace Over Perfection: The radical choice to prioritize calm over control in family life.
  • Work-Family Alignment: Building sustainable boundaries between career demands and home presence.
  • Modeling Surrender: Teaching children that releasing control is not weakness — it is the deepest form of strength.
