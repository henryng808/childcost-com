Published in July 2026, Why We Fight: How to Have Great Arguments and Heal Your Relationships by Kim Polinder is the book that every family needs — and that every parent should read with their teenager. The Conflict ROI

Polinder identifies the "conflict dividend" — the counterintuitive truth that well-managed arguments strengthen relationships far more than avoidance ever could. In a 2026 world where families are under unprecedented stress, Why We Fight is a masterclass in teaching children that disagreement is not the end of connection — it is the beginning of deeper understanding. The book provides scripts, frameworks, and practices that families can implement immediately.

Lessons for Every Family:

  • Fighting Well: The four rules of productive family conflict that children absorb and carry into their own relationships.
  • Repair After Rupture: Why the apology is the most important parenting skill — and how to model it.
  • Intergenerational Patterns: Breaking the cycles of destructive conflict that echo across generations.
