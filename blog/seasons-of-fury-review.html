Seasons of Fury: Four Muslim Families Across Generations

ChildCost Editorial Team
August 2, 2026 • 12 min read

One of the most anticipated nonfiction releases of August 2026, Seasons of Fury by Rozina Ali is a narrative history that follows four Muslim families through the post-9/11 era. Winner of awards from the NYT Magazine, Ali weaves intimate family stories into a sweeping account of resilience, faith, and the quiet heroism of parents who hold their children steady when the world turns hostile.

The Resilience Premium

Ali identifies the "historical burden" carried by families who must raise children in a climate of suspicion. In a 2026 world still shaped by the aftershocks of those decades, Seasons of Fury is essential reading for any parent who has wondered how to protect a child's sense of self when the broader culture questions their belonging. The book is both a work of journalism and a profound meditation on the architecture of family resilience.

Lessons for All Families:

  • The Shelter of Story: How families use narrative to build identity strong enough to withstand external pressure.
  • Intergenerational Memory: The cost of forgetting — and the gift of remembering — across decades of family history.
  • Quiet Heroism: The everyday parenting decisions that compound into a child's lifelong resilience.

The ChildCost Verdict

Seasons of Fury is a monumental achievement — a book that belongs on every family's shelf as a reminder that the most radical act of parenting is to keep love steady when the world will not.
