American Hagwon: A Family Saga of Secrets and Betrayal

ChildCost Editorial Team
October 6, 2026 • 10 min read

Released September 29, 2026, American Hagwon is the long-awaited new novel from Min Jin Lee, the author of Pachinko and Free Food for Millionaires. Clint Smith calls it "an enthralling family saga rich with intrigue, secrets, and betrayal… a literary triumph written in the most exquisite prose." Lee's subject has always been the family as an economic engine — the generations who sacrifice, the children who are expected to convert that sacrifice into achievement, and the price everyone pays when the arithmetic doesn't work out. In a season when governments are fighting over who deserves subsidised care, American Hagwon returns the conversation to its true subject: the families behind the numbers.

The Achievement Premium

Lee identifies the "achievement premium" — the hidden price families pay when education becomes the family project, and love is expressed as expectation. Her saga moves with the patience of generations, tracing how secrets and betrayals seeded in one era bloom in the next. For parents in 2026, squeezed between tutoring costs, childcare cliffs, and their children's futures, American Hagwon is both a mirror and a warning: the family that invests everything can still bankrupt itself in ways no ledger records.

Lessons for Families:

  • The Cost of the Climb: How the pursuit of achievement reshapes what a family is for.
  • Secrets Across Generations: Why what families hide from each other becomes the family's real inheritance.
  • Love as Expectation: The difference between pushing a child and burdening one.

The ChildCost Verdict

American Hagwon is Min Jin Lee at her most commanding — a family saga of ambition, betrayal, and the exquisite prose that has made her one of the essential novelists of our time. It is the book to read in a year when the world rediscovered that childcare is infrastructure.
