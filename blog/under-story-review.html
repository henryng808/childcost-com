Under Story: The Epic Love Story of Family

ChildCost Editorial Team
September 7, 2026 • 10 min read

Released September 1, 2026, Under Story is the new novel from Chloe Benjamin, the author of The Immortalists — the beloved saga of four siblings who learn the dates of their own deaths. Ann Napolitano calls Under Story "wondrous, wildly ambitious, an epic love story," and early readers are already placing it beside the great American family novels. For ChildCost readers, it arrives in a week when Washington is debating who counts as a family — and which families deserve public support. Benjamin's answer, told through the generations of one unforgettable family, is that the deepest story of any family is love: inherited, tested, and chosen anew.

The Inheritance Premium

Benjamin identifies what we might call the "inheritance premium" — the hidden weight families carry from the generations that came before, and the hidden power they gain by naming it. In a 2026 defined by childcare cliffs, fertility debates, and the politics of who "really" works, Under Story is a reminder that a family is not a policy category but a story: one that begins before we are born and continues after we are gone.

Lessons for Families:

  • The Family as Epic: Why the small choices of one generation become the destiny of the next.
  • Love as Inheritance: How the love we receive — and the love we refuse to pass on — shapes everything.
  • Choosing Your Story: The power of families to rewrite the narratives they inherit rather than repeat them.

The ChildCost Verdict

Under Story is Chloe Benjamin's most ambitious novel yet — an epic, tender argument that family love is both our inheritance and our choice. In a season of family-policy battles, it is the book that remembers what the fight is actually for.
