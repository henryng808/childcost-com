Appraisals: Claire Boyles on What We Pass On

ChildCost Editorial Team
August 3, 2026 • 10 min read

Named one of the LA Times' "10 Books to Read in August" and a Most Anticipated book of Fall 2026 by Debutiful, Appraisals by Claire Boyles is a stunning new voice in fiction that asks the question every family must face: what do we value, and what does that say about what we pass on? Boyles weaves stories of inheritance — financial, emotional, and cultural — into a narrative that redefines what it means to leave something behind.

The Value Premium

Boyles identifies the "appraisal gap" — the distance between what we tell our children is valuable and what our actions demonstrate. In a 2026 world where economic inequality is the single greatest predictor of child outcomes, Appraisals is a masterclass in the economics of family values. The novel asks us to examine what we appraise — and what our appraisal says about us.

Lessons for Families:

  • What We Value: The radical act of examining what we teach our children to treasure.
  • Inheritance Beyond Money: The emotional and cultural legacies that shape children more profoundly than any trust fund.
  • The Family Ledger: Understanding the invisible balance sheet of giving and receiving across generations.

The ChildCost Verdict

Appraisals is a bold, wise, and deeply moving debut. Boyles proves that the most important inheritance we leave our children is not our money — it is our values, and the courage to examine them.
