Better Than We Found It: Raising Children for the World We Want

ChildCost Editorial Team
July 30, 2026 • 12 min read

One of the most discussed parenting books of summer 2026, Better Than We Found It: Conversations with Parents About Justice, Joy, and Raising the Next Generation collects the voices of parents, educators, and activists who are reimagining what it means to raise children in a time of cascading crises. The book is at once a practical guide and a moral compass.

The Justice Premium

The contributors identify the "justice premium" — the profound long-term returns of raising children who understand that the world they inherit is not fixed, but changeable. In a 2026 context where children are facing a future of climate disruption, AI transformation, and democratic fragility, Better Than We Found It argues that the most important parenting skill is teaching children to be agents of repair. From dinner-table conversations to community organizing, the book provides scripts, practices, and frameworks for families at every stage.

Lessons for Families:

  • Intergenerational Repair: Teaching children to see themselves as healers of inherited systems, not just inheritors of them.
  • Joy as Resistance: Why protecting a child's capacity for delight is a radical act in anxious times.
  • Collective Agency: How families can move beyond individual resilience to build community-level change.

The ChildCost Verdict

Better Than We Found It is an essential volume for any parent who has ever looked at the world and wondered: what can I possibly do? The answer, this book proves, is: raise a child who will do better — and start now.
