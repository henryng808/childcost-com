The In-Between: A Memoir of Family, Community, and Possibility

ChildCost Editorial Team
August 2, 2026 • 9 min read

One of August 2026's most anticipated memoirs, The In-Between by Katie Van Heidrich is a story for anyone who has ever lived in the space between struggle and hope. Van Heidrich chronicles a childhood marked by housing insecurity, economic instability, and the quiet persistence of a mother who refused to let circumstances define her children's futures.

The Poverty Premium

Van Heidrich identifies the "in-between tax" — the hidden cost that children pay when they grow up in economic precarity: the lost opportunities, the stress that rewires developing brains, and the extraordinary resilience that emerges when community fills the gaps that systems leave behind. In a 2026 world where 23 million more children are at risk of poverty, The In-Between is a call to action dressed as a memoir — and a masterclass in the economics of hope.

Lessons for Families:

  • Community as Survival: How networks of care — neighbors, teachers, church members — become lifesaving infrastructure for families in crisis.
  • The Persistence Premium: The compounding returns of a parent who refuses to give up, even when every system has given up on them.
  • Possibility as Inheritance: The radical act of raising children to believe in futures they cannot yet see.

The ChildCost Verdict

The In-Between is a luminous, necessary memoir. Van Heidrich proves that the most valuable thing a parent can give a child is not money — it is the unshakable belief that possibility exists, even in the spaces where it seems most absent.
