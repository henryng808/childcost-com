Second Wind: The Slow Art of Reconciliation

ChildCost Editorial Team
August 6, 2026 • 9 min read

Releasing August 2026, Second Wind by Russell is a memoir of reckoning — the author's journey to reconcile with himself and the prevailing issues of his time. With the release date landing on the anniversary of Hiroshima, its themes of forgiveness and reconciliation feel especially resonant this week. The book reads as urgent and insightful today as it was when first conceived.

The Reconciliation Premium

Russell identifies the "reconciliation premium" — the long, non-linear work of mending the relationship between who we were, who we are, and who we want to become. In a 2026 world defined by fracture — political, familial, generational — Second Wind is a masterclass in the discipline of repair. For parents, it offers a model of what it means to model reconciliation for children.

Lessons for Families:

  • Repair as Ritual: Building the practice of mending relationships before they harden into silence.
  • Self-Reconciliation First: The counterintuitive truth that we must make peace with ourselves to model peace for our children.
  • Patience with Process: Trusting the slow, imperfect work of change.

The ChildCost Verdict

Second Wind is a quiet, powerful memoir for our fractured moment. Russell proves that the second wind we seek is not found — it is practiced, breath by breath, reconciliation by reconciliation.
