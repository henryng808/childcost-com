Partita: The Greatest Romance of All

ChildCost Editorial Team
October 11, 2026 • 10 min read

Released October 6, 2026, Partita is the new novel from Barbara Kingsolver (Harper), the Pulitzer Prize-winning author of Demon Copperhead. The publisher describes it as "the story of a brief, passionate, life-changing love affair, wrapped up in what might be the greatest romance of all: our eternal human longing for art and beauty." In a week when governments on two continents are arguing over whether families can afford care, Kingsolver's reminder is quietly radical: a life is not only made of logistics. It is also made of longing.

The Longing Premium

Kingsolver identifies what we might call the "longing premium" — the part of family life that cannot be budgeted, scheduled, or optimized. In a 2026 of childcare cost calculators and subsidy taper rates, Partita restores something most parenting content ignores: the hunger for beauty, for a self beyond the provider role. Children raised by adults who still long for something are children who learn that a life can be more than a spreadsheet.

Lessons for Families:

  • Modeling Longing: Why children benefit when their parents pursue beauty, not only duty.
  • The Brief and the Lasting: How a short, passionate chapter of a life becomes permanent in the people it touches.
  • Art as Family Inheritance: The case for making room for music, painting, and books in a household budget that is already stretched.

The ChildCost Verdict

Partita is Kingsolver at her most lyrical and most human — a reminder that the greatest thing parents pass down is not money or even time, but the capacity to want something beautiful.
