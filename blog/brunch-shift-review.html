The Brunch Shift: A Daughter's Portrait of Working Motherhood

ChildCost Editorial Team
August 4, 2026 • 8 min read

One of the 10 Great New Children's Books Out in August 2026 selected by LitHub, The Brunch Shift by Adrienne Thurman, illustrated by Mags DeRoma, is a warm-hearted picture book about a little girl who wakes early on Saturday mornings to accompany her mother to work at a restaurant. The narrator watches Mama navigate the "egg-crackin', pancake-flippin', orange-juicin' brunch shift," waiting patiently through a long day of bus rides, kitchen cleanup, and other demands. It is a love letter to working mothers everywhere — and a window into the invisible labor that children witness but rarely name.

The Visibility Premium

Thurman identifies the "witness dividend" — the profound understanding that children gain when they see their parents work. In a 2026 world where childcare costs are at record highs, The Brunch Shift is a quiet, radical book that validates the invisible workforce of working mothers and the children who watch them transform labor into love.

Lessons for Families:

  • Labor as Love: Teaching children to see work as an expression of care, not just obligation.
  • The Witness Dividend: How watching a parent work builds a child's understanding of resilience and commitment.
  • Joy in the In-Between: Finding moments of connection in the rhythms of busy family life.

The ChildCost Verdict

The Brunch Shift is a luminous picture book that belongs on every family's shelf. Thurman and DeRoma prove that the quiet moments of everyday labor are the building blocks of a child's deepest understanding of love.
